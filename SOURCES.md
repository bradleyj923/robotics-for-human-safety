# Sources and editorial approach

This public collection was prepared by Bradley Shogren with AI drafting and research assistance. Personal recollections are identified as such; technical examples link to original research, public agencies, creators or company announcements. Company announcements describe what the company reports, not independent proof of every performance claim.

## What the labels mean

- **Deployed example:** equipment introduced for operational use.
- **Real-world study:** tested in actual circumstances, with results and limits in the linked study.
- **Development or research:** an active investigation or prototype.
- **Design question:** a possibility to investigate, not a capability claimed to exist.

## Core sources

- [NIST: emergency response robot performance](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/performance-emergency-response-robots)
  Turn a broad goal such as “a useful rescue robot” into capabilities that can be tested and compared.
- [NIST: first-responder location, 2026](https://www.nist.gov/blogs/taking-measure/how-thousands-tiny-dots-can-save-first-responders-lives)
  See why responder location is a whole-system challenge rather than a single-coordinate problem.
- [NIST: infrastructure-free firefighter localization](https://www.nist.gov/ctl/pscr/infrastructure-free-localization-system-firefighters)
  Explore how complementary sensors can help when satellite positioning is not enough.
- [U.S. Coast Guard: 2001 GPS performance standard](https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/gps/geninfo/2001SPSPerformanceStandardFINAL.pdf)
  A historical anchor for separating the capabilities GPS already had from the difficulties of reliable indoor use.
- [National Park Service: Mount Rainier memorial](https://www.nps.gov/mora/learn/news/in-memory.htm)
  Remember the people behind the rescue questions, with the park service’s record of those lost.
- [LAFD: Thermite RS3](https://lafd.org/news/lafd-debuts-rs3-first-robotic-firefighting-vehicle-united-states)
  See a concrete example of keeping human judgment while moving the operator away from a hazardous position.
- [AED drone-delivery clinical study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38000871/)
  Follow an intervention from airborne equipment to real-world use and clinical evaluation.
- [NASA: Squishy Robotics](https://www.nasa.gov/technology/tech-transfer-spinoffs/nasa-developed-spherical-robots-to-the-rescue/)
  Consider how a robot’s shape and resilience can enable useful sensing without sending a person first.
- [NIST: AI flashover research](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2022/08/ai-may-come-rescue-future-firefighters)
  Explore how interpreting sensor data might create useful warning time rather than merely record a hazard.
- [Lex Fridman podcast archive](https://lexfridman.com/podcast/)
  Find the original conversations and give the interviewer and guests the credit and attention their work deserves.
- [Luminary Physics AI DevDay](https://luminary.ai/devday/)
  Explore the connection between models of the physical world and engineering decisions.
- [Unitree company timeline](https://www.unitree.com/mobile/about/)
  Use a coordinated performance as a starting point for questions about control, timing and the difference between demonstrations and field conditions.

## Corrections and contributions

Open an issue with the page, the claim and a better source. A good contribution explains why the example matters, what was demonstrated, and what a curious reader might investigate next. Keep contributions constructive, nonpartisan and focused on people and evidence.

Do not add private transcripts, student contact details, identifiable patient information, private correspondence, credentials or material you do not have permission to share. Link to third-party media instead of copying it without appropriate rights. No content here provides operational rescue, medical or weapons instructions.
